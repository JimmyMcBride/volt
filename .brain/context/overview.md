Volt is a greenfield research and prototyping project for a programming language focused on AI coding models and autonomous software-engineering agents, with human review and control preserved.

## Stage

- Inception and research framing; the evidence-informed prototype decisions are recorded in [GitHub Discussion #1](https://github.com/JimmyMcBride/volt/discussions/1).
- The research initiative is promoted as [Issue #2](https://github.com/JimmyMcBride/volt/issues/2) under the [Volt v0 — Evidence-Ready Research Prototype milestone](https://github.com/JimmyMcBride/volt/milestone/1).
- [Spec #3](https://github.com/JimmyMcBride/volt/issues/3), the research evidence and evaluation protocol, is decision-complete and explicitly approved for execution.
- Specs [#4](https://github.com/JimmyMcBride/volt/issues/4), [#5](https://github.com/JimmyMcBride/volt/issues/5), and [#6](https://github.com/JimmyMcBride/volt/issues/6) remain blocked and unapproved for execution.
- The central hypothesis remains unvalidated, and the recorded v0 kernel and TypeScript/Node reference architecture are proposed contracts rather than implemented behavior.
- GitHub owns planning: Discussions for shaping, Issues for specs, and Milestones/Projects for sequencing.
- Local `.plan/` retains backend metadata and compatibility pointers only.
- Brain owns concise durable mission, status, terminology, risks, and canonical links without duplicating GitHub specs.

## Product Priorities

- Deterministic and explicit behavior.
- Machine-checkable contracts, effects, and failures.
- Structured diagnostics that support agent repair.
- Inspectable, governable agent-written programs.
- Experimental evidence before broad compiler investment.
